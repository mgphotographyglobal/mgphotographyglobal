Dubai's mix of skyline, desert, and coastline gives couples an unusually wide range of settings for wedding photography. Here are five that we return to again and again — and why they work.

## 1. Jumeirah Beach at Golden Hour

Soft sand, warm light, and the skyline in the distance make Jumeirah Beach one of the most requested settings for sunset portraits. Arrive roughly 45 minutes before sunset for the best light.

## 2. Al Fahidi Historical District

For couples wanting texture and heritage rather than skyline, Al Fahidi's wind-tower architecture and narrow lanes offer a completely different, timeless mood — particularly striking in black-and-white editing.

## 3. Dubai Marina Waterfront

Modern, glassy, and dramatic — the Marina works especially well for evening shoots where reflections and skyline lights become part of the composition.

## 4. Private Villa Courtyards

For couples who prefer privacy over public locations, a villa courtyard with good natural light can outperform any public spot — no crowds, no time pressure, and full control over styling.

## 5. Desert Dunes Just Outside the City

For a striking contrast to Dubai's urban identity, a short drive into the desert delivers uninterrupted horizons and soft, warm tones that photograph beautifully close to sunset.

## Choosing the Right Location for You

The best location depends on your priorities — privacy, skyline drama, cultural texture, or natural landscape. During planning, we help couples match a location to their vision rather than defaulting to the most "popular" spot.
