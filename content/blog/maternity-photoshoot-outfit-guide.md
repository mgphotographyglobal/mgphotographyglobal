Choosing an outfit for a maternity shoot can feel overwhelming — but a few simple principles make the decision much easier, whatever style you're drawn to.

## 1. Flowing Fabric Photographs Beautifully

Chiffon and tulle gowns move with the wind and with your body, creating soft, elegant lines that flatter every stage of pregnancy. This is why flowing gowns are the most requested option for outdoor sessions along Dubai's waterfront and beach locations.

## 2. Choose a Colour Palette, Not Just a Colour

Rather than picking a single dress colour, think in palettes:

- **Warm neutrals** (cream, camel, soft beige) — timeless, and they photograph well against Dubai's golden-hour light.
- **Jewel tones** (deep red, emerald, sapphire) — dramatic and editorial, ideal for sunset sessions.
- **Soft pastels** (lilac, blush, powder blue) — gentle and romantic, particularly popular for garden or veranda settings.

## 3. Fitted Underneath, Flowing on Top

A well-fitted underdress paired with a sheer, flowing overlay gives you the best of both — definition where you want it, movement where it matters for photographs.

## 4. Bring a Second Outfit

We recommend one flowing gown for movement-based shots and one simpler, fitted outfit (or even your partner's shirt) for quieter, more intimate portraits.

## 5. Skip Busy Patterns

Solid colours and subtle textures photograph far better than bold prints, which tend to distract from your silhouette and expression — the real subject of a maternity session.
