Newborn photography is one of the most rewarding — and most time-sensitive — sessions a family will ever book. This guide brings together everything we cover with Dubai parents during planning, from timing through to the day of the shoot itself, so you can approach your session with confidence rather than guesswork.

## Start Here: The Three Questions Every Parent Asks

Almost every conversation we have with expecting parents comes back to three things: **when** to book, **how** to prepare, and **what actually happens** during the session. This guide is organised around those three questions, with links through to a dedicated deep-dive on each.

## 1. Timing Your Session

The newborn window — when babies are still sleepy and naturally curl into the soft poses this genre is known for — is short, typically 5 to 14 days after birth. Because due dates are unpredictable, we ask parents to reserve a provisional date during pregnancy rather than after birth.

Read our full breakdown in [when to book your newborn photoshoot in Dubai](/blog/newborn-photoshoot-timing-dubai/), including what to do if your baby arrives early or late.

## 2. Preparing for the Session

A little preparation goes a long way — from keeping the room warm to feeding baby beforehand. We've put together a complete room-by-room and hour-by-hour checklist in our [newborn session preparation guide](/blog/newborn-session-preparation-guide/).

## 3. What Happens During a Home Session

Because we bring a full home-studio setup to you anywhere in Dubai, there's no need to travel with a newborn or prepare your home to look "photo ready." Our team arrives with backdrops, wraps, props and lighting, and works entirely around your baby's feeding and sleep rhythm — sessions typically run 2–3 hours to allow for feeds, changes, and settling time between setups.

## Safety Always Comes First

Every pose we use is one that keeps baby safe and supported at all times — nothing is ever left unsupported, and comfort takes priority over any individual shot. If your baby seems unsettled during a particular setup, we simply move on to the next one.
