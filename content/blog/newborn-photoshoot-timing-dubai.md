If you only remember one thing about newborn photography, remember this: timing matters more than almost anything else. The soft, curled-up poses that make newborn photography so distinctive are only possible in a narrow window — and in Dubai's fast-paced hospital-to-home routine, that window can pass before parents realise it.

## The Golden Window: 5 to 14 Days

Most professional newborn photographers, including our team, recommend shooting between **5 and 14 days** after birth. During this stage, babies are still deeply sleepy, naturally flexible, and comfortable being gently posed into the curled, womb-like shapes that define the genre.

After around three weeks, babies become more alert, their startle reflex grows stronger, and the classic "sleepy pose" shots become far harder to capture safely and comfortably.

## Why We Book During Pregnancy

Because the window is so tight, we ask expecting parents to reserve their session date during the **second trimester**. This does not lock in an exact day — it simply secures your place in our calendar. Once your baby arrives, you contact us and we confirm the exact shoot date within that 5–14 day window.

## What If Baby Arrives Early or Late?

Life rarely follows a plan, and neither do due dates.

- **Early arrivals:** We simply shift the session later, factoring in extra rest time and, if needed, any time in a neonatal unit.
- **Late arrivals:** The session moves forward. Since we already know your due month, adjusting by a few days is straightforward.

## Planning Around Dubai Life

Many of our clients are managing visiting family, recovery, and a home that isn't always ready for a full studio-style setup — which is why we bring a complete home-studio setup to you, anywhere in Dubai, rather than asking you to travel with a newborn.

## Quick Checklist for Parents

1. Reserve your provisional date in your second trimester.
2. Confirm your exact session date within 48 hours of birth.
3. Keep the room warm (newborns are photographed with minimal clothing).
4. Feed baby shortly before the session — a full, sleepy baby is easiest to pose.
5. Don't stress about the house being "photo-ready" — we bring everything.
